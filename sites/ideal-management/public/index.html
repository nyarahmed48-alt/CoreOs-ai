What we do

Six services, one accountable team.

Take the whole thing or a single piece of it. Every engagement gets one named contact who knows your business — not a ticket queue.

Recruitment & Staffing

Permanent, contract and temporary hires. We screen against the job you actually need doing, then send a shortlist you can act on — not a stack of CVs to sort yourself.

HR Management

Contracts, handbooks, appraisals, grievances and exits — handled properly and documented, whether or not you have an HR person of your own.

Payroll & Compliance

Payroll run on time, every cycle, with the filings and records that keep an inspection uneventful. Errors are ours to find before you do.

Training & Development

Onboarding that sticks, supervisor training, and skills programmes built around the gaps your appraisals keep surfacing.

Business Consulting

Org structure, job architecture and pay bands. We tell you where the headcount plan breaks before you hire into the crack.

Managed Outsourcing

Whole functions — reception, back office, field teams — staffed, supervised and reported on by us, against service levels you set.

How it works

Four steps. No mystery in the middle.

  1. 01

    We listen

    A short call about the role or the problem. What good looks like, what it pays, and what has gone wrong before.

  2. 02

    We search

    The CV Pool first, then targeted outreach. Every candidate is spoken to before they reach your inbox.

  3. 03

    You choose

    A shortlist with notes on each person — strengths, gaps, and why they are on the list. We coordinate the interviews.

  4. 04

    We stay

    Offer, onboarding and the payroll behind it. We check in at 30, 90 and 180 days, and we act on what we hear.
